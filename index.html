Packages & Libraries

OPEN-SOURCE TOOLING FOR THE NEUROIMAGING COMMUNITY

Electrophysiology (EEG/iEEG)

01 / PREPROCESSING

xeeg_kit

Multi-stage artifact removal for 280-channel EEG. Integrates MEEGKit (ASR + STAR + SNS) with ICLabel deep-learning classification. Parallel batch processing with per-subject logging.

View Repo

02 / SOURCE RECONSTRUCTION

lcmv_xtra

LCMV beamforming on fsaverage space with automated BEL 280 coregistration. Time courses from DiFuMo (512), Glasser+Tian (414), and CIMT Unified (448) atlases. Debiased WPLI connectivity across eight frequency bands.

View Repo

03 / STATISTICAL INFERENCE

lcmv_stats

Group-level permutation tests, GPDC directional connectivity, cluster-based time-frequency correction, and automated reporting — optimized for the CIMT Unified Atlas (448 ROIs).

View Repo

Brain Imaging (fMRI)

04 / PARCELLATION

gt_map

TR-flexible Glasser+Tian parcellator — 360 cortical and 54 subcortical regions. Resamples before standardizing; no temporal filtering. Built for multi-site studies (ABIDE, UK Biobank, ADHD-200).

View Repo

05 / VISUALIZATION

cimt_atlas

Interactive 3D visualization of the CIMT 448-ROI Atlas. Hierarchical cascade filter (System, Hemisphere, Sub-system) with interactive 3D mesh rendering in the browser.

Open App

06 / TEACHING & REPRODUCIBILITY

neuro_ai

Graduate-level teaching materials for reproducible computational neuroscience. Covers DWI, EEG, fMRI, LFP, and BCI.

View Repo

Ongoing Research

07 / FOUNDATION MODEL

BBT

Multivariate time series transformer for fMRI-based biomarker discovery, trained on Glasser+Tian (414 ROI) parcellated time series. Rotary embeddings and grouped-query attention for diagnostic classification across ADHD, ASD, and UK Biobank cohorts. Transfer learning, permutation-based interpretability, single-subject diagnosis.

View Repo

08 / CLINICAL NEUROSCIENCE

Parkinson's Disease EEG

Three complementary experiments (bimanual motor task, resting-state, intracranial LFP) validating LCMV source-localized EEG against gold-standard LFP STN recordings. Frequency-specific connectivity changes across theta through high-gamma bands in motor-basal-executive networks.
